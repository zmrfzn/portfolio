Background

Lead Developer Relations Engineer with 9+ years of experience across full-stack engineering and developer enablement, specialising in observability and cloud-native distributed systems.

Currently at New Relic, I drive DevRel initiatives across India and APJ — designing workshops, delivering conference talks, creating technical content, and building ecosystem partnerships that enable thousands of practitioners.

Previously, I've built scalable front-end frameworks at Mitel Networks, containerization tooling at Aspect Software, and full-stack platforms at Moody's Analytics. I also co-founded a startup in Mumbai. For the full story, visit my LinkedIn.
